Questions

الأسئلة الشائعة

 

Who can sell on WARD?

Women-owned businesses in the UAE, from home kitchens to registered brands. Every seller is reviewed before joining.

I'm not good with phones. Can I still join?

Yes! Press the Warda button at the bottom of the page and talk to her. She will guide you step by step in Arabic.

Does it cost anything to join the First 100?

Applying is free. Founding sellers get special pricing that stays with them for life.

When does WARD launch?

We are onboarding our founding sellers in the UAE now. Join the waiting list and we will tell you first.

Is WARD coming to my country?

Yes. After the UAE we plan to expand across the GCC and the Arab world. Join the waiting list and choose your country.

Can my company order in bulk or sponsor WARD?

Yes. Send a request in the "For companies & sponsors" section and our team will contact you.

نستقبل المورّدات المؤسِّسات في الإمارات الآن. سجّلي في قائمة الانتظار وسنبلغك أولاً.

هل سيصل ورد إلى بلدي؟

نعم، بعد الإمارات نخطط للتوسع في الخليج والوطن العربي. سجّلي في قائمة الانتظار واختاري بلدك.
