About this website

wardworld.org is the pre-launch website of WARD, a marketplace for women-owned businesses launching in the United Arab Emirates. At this stage the website collects applications and expressions of interest. No products are sold and no payments are taken on this website.

Seller applications

Applying to join the First 100 founding sellers is free. Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance. WARD reviews each application and may ask for information to confirm that the business is woman-owned. Founding-seller benefits and pricing will be confirmed in writing before any seller agreement is signed.

Gift vouchers, companies and sponsors

Forms for gift vouchers, corporate orders, sponsorship and partnerships register your interest only. They are not a purchase, an order or a binding offer. Any order, voucher or sponsorship will be agreed separately in writing.

Warda, our voice assistant

Warda is an automated assistant. Her answers are general guidance, not legal, tax or financial advice, and she can make mistakes. For decisions about your business, please speak to a qualified professional or contact the WARD team.
